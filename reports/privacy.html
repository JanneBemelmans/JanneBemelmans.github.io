Privacy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The tool signs in to its owner's Google account only, with the drive.file permission. It can only reach the folders and files it creates itself. It does not read, keep or share any other account data.

The reports it creates stay in the owner's Google Drive and are shared only with people the owner chooses. Nothing is sold or passed on to anyone else.

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account settings (Security → Third-party apps with account access).
