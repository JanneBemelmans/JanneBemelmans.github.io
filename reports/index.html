Reports

A small private tool that prepares routine reports and stores them in its owner's Google Drive.

It is not a public service. Only its owner uses it, and no one else can sign in to it.

Google permissions

The tool uses a single Google permission, drive.file: it can create folders and files in its owner's Drive and update the files it created itself. It cannot see or change anything else.
