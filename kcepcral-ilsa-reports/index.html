KCEP-CRAL ILSA reports

KCEP-CRAL ILSA reports is a small internal tool for the KCEP-CRAL/ILSA baseline household survey in Kenya. Every night it checks newly collected survey data for data-quality problems and saves the resulting validation reports to the Google Drive of the project lead.

It is not a public service: only the research team uses it, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What the tool does with Google

The tool uses one Google permission, drive.file. With it, the tool can create folders and files in the project lead's Google Drive and update the files it created itself. It cannot see, open or change any other file in that Drive.
