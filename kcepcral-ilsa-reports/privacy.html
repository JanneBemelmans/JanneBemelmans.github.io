Privacy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Google account data

The tool signs in to the project lead's own Google account only. It uses the drive.file permission, which limits it to the folders and files the tool creates itself. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data: no email, contacts, calendar or other Drive files.

Survey data

The validation reports contain information from the KCEP-CRAL/ILSA baseline survey. That information is collected, processed and shared under the research project's own data-protection arrangements and consent procedures. The reports are stored in the project lead's Google Drive and shared only with named members of the research team.

Sharing and retention

The tool does not sell or pass data to anyone. Reports stay in Google Drive until the research team deletes them. Access for the tool can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account settings (Security → Third-party apps with account access).
